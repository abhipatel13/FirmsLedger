FirmsLedger is an independent directory of verified US manufacturers. Buyers browse by capability — metal fabrication, PCB manufacturing, plastics, packaging and more — and filter by state to find suppliers near them. Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, with contact details, capabilities and location on a single profile page, so procurement teams can shortlist without wading through pay-to-play ad placements.

**Bing Places**
A US manufacturer directory organized by capability and state. FirmsLedger lists fabricators, PCB shops, packaging suppliers and other industrial producers, each profile verified before publication, so buyers can find and contact a supplier directly instead of routing through a lead broker.

**Apple Business Connect**
Directory of verified American manufacturers, searchable by what they make and where they are. FirmsLedger profiles carry capabilities, location and direct contact details for each supplier.

**BBB**
